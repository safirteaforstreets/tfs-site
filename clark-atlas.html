The quarter mile around your door.

We start where people actually walk: the blocks between the parking, the transit stop and your entrance.

Walked, street by street.

A trained surveyor covers the whole zone on foot, with a record of what was covered and when.

Each finding, photographed and placed.

Each one is pinned to its spot and checked by a person before it counts.

It adds up to one number.

A readiness score from 0 to 100, the few findings that move it, and who to call about each one.
